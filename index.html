Киіз үй фразеологиясы

Phraseological units built around the structural parts of the yurt. Each part of the dwelling became a metaphor for character, family and society.

Ethnotranslation: Kazakh → English

A literal calque destroys the cultural meaning of yurt metaphors. Functional-conceptual equivalence and contextual explication keep it.

№Kazakh phraseologismLiteral (calque, incorrect)Functional ethnotranslation (correct)Linguacultural explanation
1Шаңырағың ортасына түскір!
Shañyrağıñ ortasyna tüskır!		May your wooden circle fall into the middle!May your house and family lineage suffer total destruction!An ethno-curse: remaining without descendants, total annihilation of the domestic microcosm.
2Қара шаңырақ
Qara shañyraq		Black wooden domeAncestral home / the main lineage hearth“Qara” means not colour but sacredness, ancient origin and the venerated hearth of the lineage.
3Уығы шайқалу
Uyğı shayqalu		To have one’s yurt poles shakenTo experience severe instability in one’s life or familyThe uyq is the main support of the dome; the phrase depicts losing foundational support.
4Босаға аттау
Bosağa attau		To step over the thresholdTo enter a new family through marriage / to cross a sacred boundaryMarks the transition and adaptation of a bride (kelın) into her new family.
5Шаңырақ көтеру
Shañyraq kötörü		To raise a wooden domeTo establish a family / lay the foundation of a householdConceptual adaptation: “shañyraq” is a synecdoche for the family as an institution.
6Керегесі кең, босағасы берік
Keregesi keñ, bosağasy berık		Its lattice is wide, its doorpost is firmA household of broad hospitality, stability and unbreakable cohesionDescriptive translation: kerege = openness, bosağa = internal stability.
7Төрдегі басың босағаға домаламасын
Tördegı basyñ bosağağa domalamasyn		May your head at the tör not roll to the thresholdMay you never fall from honour into humiliationCultural equivalent: the tör–bosağa hierarchy is mapped onto social status.

Three strategies

1. Conceptual adaptationReplace the image with an English institutional metaphor (establish a family).
2. Descriptive translationUnpack the ethnopsychological meaning with cultural annotation.
3. Functional equivalentMap the Kazakh spatial hierarchy onto English concepts of status and honour.

The Yurt in Kazakh Phraseology

Language as a reflection of nomadic culture

М.Құсайынов атындағы Ақтөбе облыстық дарынды балаларға арналған мамандандырылған лицей-интернатЖетекшісі: Баешева Жанаргул Айсагимовна, педагог-зерттеуші, магистр

Problem

For the Kazakh people the yurt was never only a dwelling: it was a micro-model of the cosmos, the family hierarchy and the state. Phraseological units built on its parts — shañyraq, kerege, uyq, bosağa — encode the nation's ethical and philosophical norms. When such units are translated into English word for word, this cultural code is lost.

Aim

To reveal how the structural parts of the yurt are transformed into phraseological units of the Kazakh language, and to determine adequate strategies for translating them into English.

Methods

Ethnolinguistic analysisYurt lexicon and its metaphorical meanings.
Linguacultural analysisTör, bosağa, oshaq as a model of social hierarchy.
Comparative translation analysisLiteral calque vs functional ethnotranslation.
3D visualisationEach part of the model is linked to its phraseology.

Key conclusions

ШаңырақSymbol of peace, divine benevolence and continuity of generations.
КерегеSymbol of unity and structural equity.
УықSymbol of responsibility and duty.
БосағаSymbol of humility and virtue.

Just as the yurt frame is built without a single metal nail, the phraseology of its parts forms a system bound together by the inner laws of the language.
